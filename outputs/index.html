About R&T Global Logistics

Three decades of family experience. One new-generation company.

R&T Global Logistics is a registered Indian logistics company headquartered in New Delhi, offering rail parcel services, road transport, sea and air freight, warehousing and last-mile distribution across the country.

The company is new. The experience behind it is not. For more than thirty years, our family has worked in the movement of goods across India — at railway parcel counters, on loading bays, inside warehouses and on the road. That is where R&T began: not in a boardroom, but in the daily discipline of making sure a consignment reaches the right place, in the right condition, on the right day.

What has changed is the approach — clearer communication, faster response, transparent pricing and a single point of accountability from booking to final handover. What has not changed is the standard the family has always worked to.

Today we serve retail and FMCG brands, manufacturers, pharmaceutical suppliers, e-commerce sellers and industrial businesses, supported by a nationwide rail network, our own operations team in Delhi and a trusted partner network across India.

Our visionTo become India's most dependable logistics partner for businesses that cannot afford delays or damage.
Our missionTo make every shipment simpler, safer and more transparent through disciplined handling and honest pricing.
Leadership
RT Raunak TiwariFounder & Chief Executive Officer

Raunak Tiwari founded R&T Global Logistics at the age of 20, formalising a business his family has been part of for over thirty years.

Having spent his early years around railway parcel offices, warehouses and dispatch operations, he leads the company with a hands-on understanding of how goods actually move in India — and a clear intent to bring more structure, technology and transparency to a traditionally unorganised industry.

“I grew up watching my family treat every parcel as if it belonged to them. My job is to keep that standard and build a proper system around it — so our customers always know where their goods are and who is responsible for them.”

Raunak Tiwari, Founder & CEO
