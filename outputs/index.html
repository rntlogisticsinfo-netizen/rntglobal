About R&T Global Logistics

New company. Thirty years of hands on the cargo.

R&T Global Logistics is a registered Indian logistics company headquartered in New Delhi, moving goods by rail, road, sea and air with warehousing and last-mile support across the country.

The family has worked at railway parcel counters, loading bays and warehouses for more than three decades. What is new is the structure around it — clearer communication, faster response and a single point of accountability.

“I grew up watching my family treat every parcel as if it belonged to them. My job is to keep that standard and build a proper system around it.”

Raunak Tiwari, Founder & CEO
