Our story

Experience that stays practical.

R&T Global Logistics is a registered Indian logistics company run by people who learned this work on the platform and the loading dock — not from a manual. Today that experience runs across railway parcel operations, road transport and warehousing.

RT
Founder & CEO Raunak Tiwari

Started young, learned on the floor.

Raunak dropped out of formal studies early and went straight into the work — parcel counters, railway platforms, loading docks and long nights at the warehouse. What he could not learn in a classroom, he picked up from consignments, drivers and customers who expected their goods to arrive on time.

That hands-on start is still how R&T runs. He is personally involved in operations, keeps growing the network station by station and warehouse by warehouse, and holds the team to one standard: own the consignment until it is delivered.

“Every parcel belongs to someone who is waiting for it. That is the whole job.”
